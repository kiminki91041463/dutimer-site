Good to know

A few useful details before you get back to your day.

How does Dutimer work?

Set a duration and preview your waffle, pizza, or stew before starting. The duration automatically selects a food. Swipe the preview to choose another food, or use the gear button to adjust food time ranges.

Why does the app ask for alarm permission?

Dutimer uses Apple’s system alarm to let you know when your timer ends. Alarm permission is required to start a timer. If you denied permission, enable alarms for Dutimer in your device’s Settings, then return to the app.

How do I pause, cancel, or restart?

While the timer is running, tap Pause. The paused controls let you resume, cancel, or restart. Restart begins the same food and original duration again. At completion, use Done to finish the session.

Can I check my timer on the Lock Screen?

The Live Activity shows the remaining time and provides Pause, Resume, and Cancel controls. The interactive 3D food scene stays inside the app.

What should I include in a support email?

Tell us your Dutimer version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and what happened. A screenshot is optional. Please leave out personal or sensitive information that is unrelated to the issue.

Which devices are supported?

Dutimer requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. No account or login is required.
