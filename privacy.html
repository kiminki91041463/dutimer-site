Dutimer is a visual countdown timer developed by INKI KIM. This policy explains the information used by the app, purchases, food downloads, this website, and support. The payment and download sections describe features prepared for Dutimer 2.0 and apply when those features are available in your installed version.

The app

Dutimer does not require signup or ask for your name or email address to use the app. Your timer activity and settings are not sent to developer-operated servers. The app does not include an advertising SDK or automatically collect an advertising identifier. Purchase processing and purchase analytics are described below.

Your timer state, selected food, food time-range settings, app preferences, and alarm state are stored on your device to restore your session and settings. In Dutimer 2.0, waffle is included with the app; other food packs are downloaded from our server.

Dutimer uses Apple’s system alarm and Live Activity features to show your timer and alert you when it ends. Alarm permission is required to start a timer. You can manage that permission in your device’s Settings.

Purchases & restoration

Apple processes in-app payments. Dutimer does not receive your payment card details. We use RevenueCat and our server to verify purchases, manage subscriptions and food credits, restore access, and handle refunds and duplicate transactions.

To recognize purchases without a Dutimer login, we derive a pseudonymous customer identifier from Apple’s signed app transaction. Our server stores this identifier, the Apple app transaction identifier, and purchase, refund, credit, and permanent food ownership records. These identifiers link records for restoration; they do not contain a name or email address supplied to Dutimer.

RevenueCat receives the customer identifier and purchase history for purchase functionality and purchase analytics. The app may establish a purchase session and synchronize these records when it starts or returns to the foreground, before you open checkout or make a new purchase. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

Food downloads & security

Cloudflare hosts our download service, food packs, previews, and server records. Requests to the server provide the requested food or media and the information needed to check download access. Paid food access is checked against verified purchases or an active subscription.

We use Apple App Attest to check that requests come from the authentic Dutimer app. Security records include the App Attest key identifier, public key, attestation receipt, and request counter. Apple’s device verification identifier and signed purchase proofs are sent for verification. Request IP addresses are used to limit abusive traffic. These checks protect downloads, purchase records, and restoration. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Storage & deletion

Timer settings, downloaded food files, cached access information, and pending purchase operations are stored locally. App Attest security information is also stored in Apple’s device-only Keychain. Removing a downloaded food removes its local files, not your purchase rights. Canceling a timer ends the current timer without erasing food time-range settings.

Deleting the app removes its local app data, but Keychain items may remain. It does not delete purchase and security records on our server or purchase records held by RevenueCat or Apple. Our server retains purchase, credit, ownership, and security records for restoration and fraud prevention; these records currently have no automatic expiration or in-app deletion feature. Contact us for privacy questions or requests concerning your information.

Device backups and system services are managed by Apple according to your device settings and Apple’s policies. We do not operate a cloud backup or synchronization service for your timers and timer settings.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Please include only information needed for support.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request, related follow-up, or legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing us. We do not sell support information or use it for advertising.

Support email is handled through Outlook.com. Microsoft processes email as the email service provider under its Privacy Statement.

This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, cookies, or browser storage to these pages.

GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not visitors are signed in to GitHub. That hosting activity is separate from data used by the Dutimer app. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Apple services & beta testing

Apple operates the App Store, TestFlight, and device-level services under its own policies. When you use TestFlight or choose to share feedback or diagnostics with Apple, Apple may make feedback or diagnostic information available to the developer. We use information made available to us to investigate issues and improve the app.

For information about Apple’s processing and available choices, see Apple’s privacy information and your device settings.
